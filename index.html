01 / Proof

The folded polynomial.

The identity sin²x + cos²x = 1 means you never compute both values. Compute one, derive the other. We implemented Silas Lock's folded polynomial: reduce any angle to [-π/4, π/4], evaluate one tiny polynomial, derive the complement via quadrant symmetry.
